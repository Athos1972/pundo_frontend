# Connect Pundo to Your AI Assistant

You ask your AI assistant: "Where can I find Russian honey in Larnaca?" — and instead of a guess, it comes back with real shops and prices from Pundo. This isn't a future feature. It works today.

## What is the Pundo MCP Server?

MCP stands for **Model Context Protocol** — an open standard that lets AI agents pull data from external sources. Pundo runs its own MCP server at `mcp.pundo.cy`.

In plain terms: your AI assistant can search Pundo directly, without you opening the app.

<InfoBox type="info">
  The MCP server provides read-only access — search for products, shops and categories. No ordering, no writing.
</InfoBox>

## Connect in three steps

No API key to copy, no technical setup required. All you need is a Pundo account.

<StepList steps={[
  "Open your AI agent (e.g. Claude Desktop) and go to Settings → MCP → Add Server",
  "Enter the URL: https://mcp.pundo.cy — that's it",
  "On first connection, a browser window opens. Log in with your Pundo account and click 'Allow'"
]} />

Once authorised, the connection is saved. You won't be asked to log in again.

<InfoBox type="tip">
  You can disconnect at any time under **My Account → AI Agents** — useful if you're passing on a device.
</InfoBox>

## What can you ask?

Once Pundo is connected, your agent can handle questions like:

- "Which shops in Larnaca sell organic olive oil?"
- "How does the price of halloumi compare across different shops?"
- "Show me pet food near me"
- "Is there a Russian grocery store in Limassol?"

The agent searches Pundo in real time and returns shop names, addresses and prices — right in the chat.

## Usage limits

The Pundo MCP server is free to use. There's a fair-use limit of **500 requests per day** per account — well above what normal use requires.

<InfoBox type="info">
  The limit applies per Pundo account, not per device. You can use the same account across multiple devices.
</InfoBox>

## Managing your connections

Under **My Account → AI Agents** you can see which clients are connected and when they were last active. Disconnect any of them with a single click.
